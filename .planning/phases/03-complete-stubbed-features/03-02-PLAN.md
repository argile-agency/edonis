<objective>
Backend for the public contact form: `POST /contact`, validated (CONTACT-01), persisted (CONTACT-02, storage half), rate-limited per IP (CONTACT-03), with a honeypot. E-mail notification is plan 03-04; the page wiring and GDPR are plan 03-05.

Decisions taken here (Claude's discretion per CONTEXT.md):
- **No `tenant_id` on `contact_messages` (research A1, decided).** CLAUDE.md asks for `tenant_id` on tenant-aware tables, but no table in the repo has one and `app/` never resolves a tenant, so the column could not be filled correctly. Adding it later is one migration.
- **Throttle 5 POSTs per 10 minutes per IP, then blocked 15 minutes (A6).** Invalid and honeypot posts also consume, which is wanted against abuse.
- **Honeypot field `website`**, read from the validated payload (never `request.input`, which fails `check:debt`). A filled honeypot gets the normal success flash and stores nothing.
- **Plaintext name and e-mail (A4)**, consistent with `users.email` / `users.full_name`. CR/LF rejected in `name` and `subject` (header-injection belt for 03-04).
- **Controller**: method `storeContact` on the existing `PagesController` (it already serves `GET /contact`); no new controller.
- **Proxy IP caveat (research OQ2, Pitfall 7)**: no `trustProxy` change in this phase. Behind Infomaniak's ingress `request.ip()` may be the proxy IP, which would make this limit (and login/register) site-wide. Recorded as an accepted risk and a HUMAN-UAT follow-up (see threat model and output).

Output: migration, model, validator, controller method, route, throttle, `contact.spec.ts`.
</objective>
