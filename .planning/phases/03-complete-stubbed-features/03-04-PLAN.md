<objective>
Notify the configured address after each stored contact message (CONTACT-02, notification half).

Mechanism (Claude's discretion, decided per research): **nodemailer 10 used directly behind a small `MailService`**, not `@adonisjs/mail`. `@adonisjs/mail@10` needs AdonisJS 7 and Node 24; `@adonisjs/mail@9` pins nodemailer 6, which has open moderate/high advisories and would fail the repo's Dependency Review job (`fail-on-severity: moderate`). Do NOT run `node ace add @adonisjs/mail` and do NOT add `@types/nodemailer` (types ship in-package).

Behaviour:
- Persist first (03-02), then send inline with short timeouts (connection 10 s, greeting 10 s, socket 15 s; nodemailer defaults are 120 s / 30 s / 600 s), inside a try/catch. Success sets `notified_at`.
- "Not configured" (no `SMTP_HOST`, no sender, or no `contactEmail`) is a logged warning, not an error. Failures are logged with the message id only, never name, e-mail or body.
- `From` = the authenticated mailbox (`MAIL_FROM_ADDRESS`, else `SMTP_USERNAME`), `Reply-To` = visitor. Sending `From: visitor` would fail SPF/DMARC on Infomaniak. Text body only.

Output: dependency, `MailService`, env schema and docs, notification in `storeContact`, notification tests.
</objective>
