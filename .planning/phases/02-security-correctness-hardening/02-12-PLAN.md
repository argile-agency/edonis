<objective>
This plan finishes the DEBT-05 inventory outside the course/enrolment controllers:
- **V15**: `users.index` takes `?limit=` with no bound, a cheap DoS.
- **V16**: `audit_logs.index` passes raw date strings to the DB, which gives a PG cast 500.
