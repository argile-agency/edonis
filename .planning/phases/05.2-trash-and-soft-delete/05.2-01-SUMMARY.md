# Phase 05.2 Plan 01: Soft-delete columns and hide-trashed hooks Summary

This plan adds three nullable trash columns (`deleted_at`, `deleted_batch`, `deleted_by_id`) to sections and contents. The migration can be rolled back without losing rows. Lucid find/fetch/paginate hooks on `CourseModule` and `CourseContent` hide trashed rows from every model read, preloads and the ContentFile many-to-many included. Raw `db.from()` reads still see them.
