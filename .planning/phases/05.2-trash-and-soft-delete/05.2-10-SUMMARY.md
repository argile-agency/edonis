## Post-deploy steps (demo.edonis.org)

- The build runs the migrations (`migration:run --force`). `db:seed` is not run; the retention defaults to 30 days.
- Optional: an Infomaniak nightly scheduled task, `cd build && node ace trash:purge`. Without it, opening a course's trash purges its expired items (lazy purge, D-12).
- After a section is purged, its assignments reappear in the gradebook without a section (D-08). Grades are never deleted.
